The Format

To keep things fair, balanced and full of new connections, no one brings a ready-made team. Every squad is built on the day through a live team draw.

Step one

Five vendor pots

Every confirmed vendor is placed into a pot based on what they do. (Our ladies' line-up has its own plan — more on that in the group.)

Step two

How a squad is built

5

Five starting players

One player drawn from each of the five pots, so every team has a DJ or band, an MC or baker, a decorator or caterer, a photographer or videographer, and a bar/other vendor.

2

Two substitute players

Subs are drawn separately and shared out evenly across teams. That makes a 7-player squad and allows rolling substitutions throughout the day.

= Balanced 7-player squads

Every team ends up the same size and the same mix — so the football is competitive and the networking is built right in.

The point of it all

Why we draw teams this way

  • ⚽ Fair and balanced
    No stacked teams — everyone has a real chance.
  • 🤝 Networking built in
    You meet vendors from every category, not just your own.
  • 🎉 Fun & competitive
    Light, not tight — a great day of sport, food and music.
  • ❤️ One community
    The UK Goan business community, together through football and friendship.
